## Corrections exposed by the audit

1. The book's Seed has six named outer elementals: Fire, Air, Earth, Water,
   Metal, and Crystal. This is the primary creator model, not merely one equal
   alternative among arbitrary labels.
2. The seventh center is Consciousness. Ether is best tested as the medium or
   firmament through which Consciousness relates the six modes and nested
   scales, rather than replacing Consciousness.
3. The single circle is first a vessel or shell. The void within it is described
   separately as a transmission medium. Port behavior should therefore be an
   interface property, not the whole definition of the circle.
4. A Vesica is generated by two circles and is described as birthing a third
   domain or universe through positive and negative spiraling relation.
5. Seed expansion produces the Flower; continued Flower expansion produces the
   inner and outer Tree. Tree routing should be derived from Flower geometry,
   not introduced as an unrelated graph.
6. The book distinguishes the Flower/Tree substrate from toroidal circulation.
   Tori describe nested flow through the architecture.
7. Chapter 6 independently supports the exact 54 plus 54 polarity pairing now
   present in the canonical core, but the book's biological port claims remain
   hypotheses requiring separate evidence.
