# M4 source and claim audit v0.1

Status: bounded source review, 24 September 2026, incorporating the
user-supplied image, four candidate links, and a subsequently pasted Google
AI answer. The image attribution and original source of the cosmological
name **multiscalar matrix mechanical model (M4)** remain unresolved. The
pasted answer supplies specific claims and social-source leads that can be
assessed provisionally. It does not establish an author's complete theory,
and this audit does not claim to disprove one.

## Source identification

Searches covered the supplied name, `multi-scalar` and `multiscale` variants,
`multiscale matrix mechanics`, the abbreviation M4, and combinations of the
distinctive image labels. The later pasted Google AI text narrows the source
trail to a named Facebook group, two displayed names, dates, and post
fragments. Those leads are recorded below. No original post, derivation,
reproducible code, or quantitative validation has yet been verified as
belonging to this cosmological image and name. This is a search limitation,
not evidence that no such source exists.

The image is the source of the image correspondences below; the pasted AI
answer is a separate source of provisional claims. Neither has been
independently confirmed as a statement by an identified M4 author. The
label spelling is retained; in particular, `NUCLEIC SHELL` is not silently
reinterpreted as a specified nuclear-shell model. Nor does a slash between
two labels establish whether the intended relation is an analogy, a scaling
law, or literal physical identity.

Unrelated uses of M4 in engineering, four-dimensional manifold notation,
and established research on multiple scalar fields do not establish this
attribution. For example, [Flodgren and Sundborg's multiscalar field-theory
paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.04954) defines algebraic structures for
renormalization-group flows; it was not identified as the source of this
diagram. Equations from an unrelated paper must not be assigned to M4.

Relabeling a shell does not predict a rotation curve, lensing map, or nuclear spectrum. |

[CERN's account of the Standard Model](https://home.web.cern.ch/science/physics/standard-model/)
identifies quarks, their color charge, and gluons as strong-force carriers.
The [Dietrich et al. (2012) measurement](https://www.nature.com/articles/nature11224)
instead reconstructs a filament between Abell 222 and 223 from weak lensing,
with supporting galaxy and X-ray observations. Lensing constrains a mass
distribution; that measurement does not identify the mass as a gluon field.
The conclusion that shape alone is insufficient is the present audit's
inference from these different observables.

The [Borexino Collaboration's 2018 paper and open data](https://borex.lngs.infn.it/papers/articles/comprehensive-measurement-of-pp-chain-solar-neutrinos/)
provide measured neutrino interaction rates from multiple reactions in the
solar proton-proton fusion chain. Any proposed alternative solar interior
must reproduce those measurements, including their uncertainty and neutrino
flavor conversion, as well as other stellar constraints. The visual inset
does not perform that comparison.

Burning shells surrounding an iron core describe a late stage of sufficiently
massive stellar evolution, not every star at every age. [NASA's stellar
evolution explanation](https://science.nasa.gov/universe/stars/) distinguishes
that sequence from the evolution of Sun-like stars. A primary example is
[Heger and Woosley (2002)](https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/0107037): their
specified Population III calculations include iron-core formation followed
by possible collapse to a black hole. Those particular stellar masses and
assumptions must not be generalized into a black hole already present inside
every ordinary star.

There are measured controls for some of these ideas' ingredients. For
example, [a Faraday-wave experiment](https://www.nature.com/articles/s42005-023-01170-8)

That is evidence for the specified driven-fluid phenomenon. The

For gravity, [the MICROSCOPE final results](https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.15487)

There is also a defined thermodynamic-gravity research lead:

offers a precise bridge between thermodynamics and gravity. It does not
derive the supplied M4 extrusion picture or establish its proposed minimum
count of Planck units. No Jacobson derivation or gravitational observation
is newly tested in this repository audit.

## Conditional black-hole-in-star models

A black hole inside a star is not categorically impossible. [Bellinger et al.
(2023)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.06782) compute stellar evolution assuming a
small primordial black hole in the center. Their results depend on the
initial black-hole mass and accretion prescription. Some modeled solar cases
remain compatible with the observations considered in that paper; this is
not a detection or evidence that all stars have such centers. The authors
propose asteroseismology as a possible discriminator of the altered internal
structure.

A follow-on investigation could reproduce a published stellar model and
compare its predicted oscillation modes with observations and a matched
model without a central black hole. Mass, composition, age, accretion physics,
and measurement uncertainties would need to be specified. **No Hawking-star
evolution, asteroseismic fit, or observational exclusion is newly computed
in this audit.**

These are separate models for separate observables. Their selection does not
