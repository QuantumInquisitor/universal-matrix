# Crystal research and the breathing Matrix Engine

Research checked 30 September 2026. This assessment accompanies the local
connected-fold experiment; it does not establish a new phase of matter.

**User clarification:** the objective is to investigate useful time-crystal
subsystems within the Matrix Engine, not to determine whether the entire engine
is a time crystal. The first concrete role is an optional timing input. See
[the subsystem experiment](time_crystal_clock_subsystem_v0.1.md) for the published
model, actual canonical-clock adapter and comparison against simpler timing
sources. Earlier diagnostic work remains a comparison tool, not a requirement
that the whole engine satisfy a time-crystal definition.

## What fits

The immediate engineering fit is a **reconfigurable spatial metamaterial**:
connected units whose deformation changes the larger structure. Spatial crystal,
quasicrystal and time-crystal order are separate hypotheses to measure. A finite
recursive fractal, an attractive symmetric projection, or a prescribed breathing
animation does not establish any of them.

| Candidate | Connection to our work | Missing evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Spatial crystal | Repeated connected cells and spatial correlations | Long-range order, diffraction and size controls on actual engine coordinates |
| Spatial quasicrystal | Possible nonperiodic order; higher-dimensional mathematical representations | Aperiodic order and diffraction, rather than just recursion or visual symmetry |
| Mechanical metamaterial | Compliant panels, hubs, deformation and recursive assembly | Material law, stable dynamics, fabrication tolerances and work/dissipation |
| Continuous time crystal | A possible collective breathing state under steady input | Emergent motion, phase selection, collective correlations and robustness |
| Discrete time crystal | Possible collective response at n drive periods, n > 1 | Stable subharmonic response across perturbations and parameters; not an imposed output period |
| Space-time crystal | Both spatial and temporal ordering | Both independent sets of tests on the same physical or simulated system |

## Evidence and limits

- The [IUCr crystal definition](https://dictionary.iucr.org/Crystal) emphasizes
  essentially sharp diffraction; its [aperiodic crystal definition](https://dictionary.iucr.org/Aperiodic_crystal)
  separates long-range order from three-dimensional lattice periodicity. These
  are better classification targets than judging the outline of a model.
- [Watanabe and Oshikawa](https://arxiv.org/abs/1410.2143) rule out the proposed
  equilibrium time-crystal order under their stated assumptions, including
  sufficiently short-range interactions. The supplied lowest-energy definition
  describes the original idea, not a general description of established driven
  time crystals. It does not provide a perpetual-motion mechanism.
- [Mi et al., Nature, 2021](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-021-04257-w)
  provide a quantum-processor discrete-time-crystal benchmark: interacting
  many-body dynamics and robustness, not merely a Fourier peak at half a drive
  frequency. Finite coherence and observation times must remain explicit.
- [Liu et al., Nature Physics, 2023](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41567-023-02023-5)
  report a nanomechanical metamaterial time crystal under constant illumination.
  This is a relevant mechanical comparison, with external power and specific
  experimental conditions. It does not validate our joints or equations.
- [Zhao and Smalyukh, Nature Materials, September 2025](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41563-025-02344-1)
  demonstrate interacting liquid-crystal solitons under constant light, including
  spontaneous phase selection and resistance to disturbances. The reported
  four-dimensional configurations combine space and time; they do not establish
  four physical spatial dimensions. Continued observation requires the driven
  conditions, and proposed applications remain proposals.
- **Newer published follow-up:** [Zhao, Zhang and Smalyukh, Nature Communications,
  24 March 2026](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-026-70880-8) report
  electrically driven classical liquid-crystal space-time order, including
  period doubling and perturbation tests. This supersedes citing their July 2025
  preprint as the latest status. Their Majorana-like structures are classical
  topological analogues, not evidence of quantum Majorana particles in our model.
- [Modular chiral origami, Nature, April 2025](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-08851-0)
  is a useful comparison for collective shape change and mechanical energy
  barriers. Similarity of geometry does not transfer its material properties.

The Instagram reel could not be retrieved; its specific visuals and statements
remain unverified. Claims of unlimited lifetime, degradation-free operation,
battery-free sensing or energy extraction are not justified by these sources.
Time quasicrystals concern temporal quasiperiodicity; that does not automatically
give a spatial quasicrystal. Photonic media with deliberately modulated optical
properties are another distinct experiment, not evidence of spontaneous breathing.

## Experiments to add to the existing queue

1. **Spatial-order diagnostic.** Compute
   `S(k,t) = |sum_j exp(i k dot r_j(t))|^2 / N` from declared physical points.
   Report vertices, cell centers and material-weighted samples separately.
   Compare matched-size periodic, quasiperiodic and randomized controls. Vary
   system size and recursion depth independently; track peak width, intensity
   and spatial correlations. Show lab coordinates and a co-moving view with
   translation, rotation and common breathing removed, while preserving the
   actual deformation and density measurements.
2. **Dynamical breathing.** Give fold and size coordinates inertia, elastic
   energy, damping, coupling and explicit power input. Integrate their motion;
   do not prescribe a sinusoidal output and then call the result emergent.
   Keep the current prescribed cycle as the comparison case.
3. **Controls before classification.** Compare frozen, prescribed, uncoupled
   damped and interacting driven models. Turn input and coupling off separately.
   Vary initial phases, disorder, perturbations, coupling and drive parameters.
   Establish timestep, duration and size convergence. Account for actuator work,
   stored energy and dissipation. An ordinary stable oscillator or synchronized
   finite array does not, by itself, establish a time-crystal phase.
4. **Combined ordering.** Measure correlations and `S(k,omega)` only after the
   spatial and dynamical controls work. For periodic driving, track both the
   drive and output and test a robust nT response. For steady driving, examine
   spontaneously selected temporal phase and collective order.

These are proposed experiments. None is marked completed by the connected-fold
clearance audit. Material dynamics, recursive coupling and moving-boundary flow
remain the next engine gates; this research does not replace the outstanding
standing-wave, calibration, halo or other experiments in the completion ledger.
