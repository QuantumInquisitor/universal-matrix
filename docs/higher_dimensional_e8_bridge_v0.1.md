# Higher-Dimensional Geometry and E8 Bridge v0.1

## Decision

The engine now distinguishes two very different questions:

1. Which regular geometries continue exactly when the ambient dimension is increased?
2. Which exceptional higher-dimensional structures have an exact construction bridge from geometry already present in the repository?

The first question has a clean answer. In dimensions five and above, the regular convex families that persist are the simplex, hypercube, and cross-polytope. The second question yields one strong exploratory extension: the E8 root polytope in eight dimensions, because the existing 24-cell already supplies an exact D4 root subsystem and published Clifford-algebra work provides an independent H3/H4-to-E8 construction route.

None of these statements assign a physical meaning to an added coordinate.

## Persistent dimension ladder

For every dimension n, the new module constructs:

| family | vertex count | central mirror | recursive coordinate projection |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| n-simplex | n + 1 | no, for n > 1 | not the same regular simplex under simple coordinate drop |
| n-hypercube | 2^n | yes | projects to the (n - 1)-cube with multiplicity two |
| n-cross-polytope | 2n | yes | projects to the lower cross-polytope plus a doubled center |

The simplex is represented by n + 1 integer vectors in the sum-zero hyperplane of R^(n+1). Every vertex has squared norm n(n+1), and every pair of distinct vertices has dot product -(n+1), so regularity is exact rather than numerical.

The hypercube and cross-polytope therefore satisfy the proposed self-mirroring requirement without modification. A lone simplex does not.

## Mirror completion of the simplex

A simplex can be made centrally mirror closed by adjoining its inverted copy.

For n = 3 this is the abstract higher-coordinate analogue of the tetrahedron plus inverted tetrahedron construction associated with the stella octangula.

The important result is that this rule does not justify calling the resulting 2(n + 1)-vertex compound an n-cross-polytope in general. In four dimensions, for example, the mirrored 4-simplex has 10 vertices while the 16-cell has 8.

The engine therefore keeps two ideas separate:

- mirror completion of a simplex,
- cross-polytope geometry.

That prevents the three-dimensional stella relation from being overgeneralized.

## Exact E8 root construction

The exploratory eight-dimensional layer uses integer coordinates scaled uniformly by two.

It contains:

- 112 roots formed by permutations of (+/-2, +/-2, 0, ..., 0);
- 128 roots in {+/-1}^8 with an even number of negative signs.

The resulting 240 vectors all have squared norm 8.

The implementation verifies:

- exactly 240 roots;
- exact closure under every root reflection;
- central inversion closure;
- 120 antipodal root pairs;
- 56 nearest neighbors for every root-polytope vertex.

The 56-neighbor result matches the known skeleton of the Gosset 4_21 polytope, the E8 root polytope.

## Existing 24-cell to E8

The current 24-cell implementation already derives 24 facet normals of the form

(+/-1, +/-1, 0, 0)

and permutations.

Those are the D4 roots. Multiplying them by two and padding four zero coordinates embeds all 24 of them exactly into the 240-root E8 coordinate set.

The test suite verifies that this embedded D4 subsystem is closed under its own reflections.

This is a real construction bridge from an existing engine layer. It is stronger than choosing E8 because both the project and E8 happen to involve the number eight.

## H3, H4, and the 600-cell route

The repository already derives H3 from the icosahedron and H4 as the 120 vertices of the 600-cell.

Pierre-Philippe Dechant gives a separate published Clifford-algebra construction in which the 240 E8 roots arise from the pinors generated by H3, while the 120 even spinors produce H4.

That published result makes the existing H3 -> H4 -> E8 direction especially relevant to this project.

This version does not yet claim that the repository's present Q(phi) H4 coordinates have been transformed into the new integer E8 coordinates by a tested bijection. That explicit coordinate map is the next gate.

## Next creator questions

1. Can the current exact H4 / 600-cell coordinates be lifted into E8 by a tested map reproducing the 240 roots without inserting the target list by hand?
2. If so, what operation supplies the required double cover in engine terms?
3. Does that map commute with central mirror, H3 reflection, and the existing micro-to-whole construction graph?
4. Can any eight-dimensional direction be derived as a state variable, or are all eight coordinates only a representation space?
5. What observable would distinguish the E8 extension from a lower-dimensional model using the same finite symmetry data?

Until those questions are answered, E8 remains an exact mathematical superstructure and not a physical ontology.
