# Platonic-solid bridge v0.1

## Decision

All five Platonic solids now belong to one exact three-dimensional reference
layer. The tetrahedron, cube, and octahedron are recovered from geometry
already used by the engine. The icosahedron and dodecahedron complete the
classification through exact golden-ratio coordinates.

Their addition is a geometry result. It does not assign an element, force,
state of consciousness, or cosmological scale to any solid.

## Exact inventory

Edges and faces are derived from coordinates rather than inserted as counts.

| Solid | Vertices | Edges | Faces | Engine relation |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| tetrahedron | 4 | 6 | 4 triangles | one parity half of the stella octangula |
| cube | 8 | 12 | 6 squares | the full outer stella vertex carrier |
| octahedron | 6 | 12 | 8 triangles | the six axial boundary gates |
| dodecahedron | 20 | 30 | 12 pentagons | new exact golden-ratio reference layer |
| icosahedron | 12 | 30 | 20 triangles | new exact golden-ratio reference layer |

Every solid has a single circumradius, a single edge length, uniform vertex
degree, regular face cycles, and Euler characteristic `V - E + F = 2`.

## Exact golden-ratio field

The new pair is represented over `Q(phi)` with

`phi^2 = phi + 1`.

The implementation stores every scalar as two rational coefficients
`a + b phi`. It therefore tests equality, distance, incidence, and dual rays
without treating rounded floating-point coordinates as proof.

The icosahedron uses cyclic placements of `(+/-1, 0, +/-phi)`. The
dodecahedron combines the eight cube vertices with cyclic placements of
`(0, +/-1/phi, +/-phi)`. Its edge squared is exactly `8 - 4 phi`.

## Duality

For every face, its exact centroid ray matches exactly one outward vertex of
the dual:

- cube and octahedron;
- dodecahedron and icosahedron;
- tetrahedron and a congruent tetrahedron.

The coordinate realization reveals an important mirror detail. The face
centers of one stella tetrahedron point toward the vertices of the other.
Thus the tetrahedron is abstractly self-dual, but the centered coordinate dual
is its mirrored companion.

## Mirror law

The cube, octahedron, dodecahedron, and icosahedron are each closed under
central inversion. One tetrahedron alone is not. Central inversion exchanges
the two disjoint tetrahedra whose union is the stella cube. This prevents a
general slogan about mirroring from erasing the exceptional behavior of the
tetrahedral layer.

## Relation to the higher-dimensional research

The icosahedral family is relevant to the published H3-to-H4 spinor route that
generates the 600-cell. Completing the five-solid inventory supplies an exact
icosahedral reference, but it does not yet prove that H3 is generated by the
engine's existing six gates or state variables. The 600-cell therefore remains
a candidate until that missing construction is established and tested.

## Evidence boundary

The contract proves regular-polyhedron coordinates, incidence, duality, and
central-mirror behavior. Historical symbolic correspondences can be recorded
as interpretation metadata later, but they must not be substituted for a
measurable physical law.
