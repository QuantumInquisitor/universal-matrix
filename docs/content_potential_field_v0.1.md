## 8. What remains unresolved

The static field equation still does not determine:

- the numerical value of (kappa_{mathcal C});
- the clock coupling (g_chi);
- the reference tick duration;
- the massive-particle action branch;
- whether (ho_{mathcal C}) is literally physical energy density;
- how changes in (chi) propagate in time.

The last point is especially important.

An instantaneous Poisson field is only a static limit.

A physical long-range theory needs a dynamical field equation with finite
propagation speed.
