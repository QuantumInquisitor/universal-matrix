## Physical normalization

[The unit audit](physical-normalization-audit.json) records six existing
subsystem conventions and their missing inputs. It references the reviewed local
combined snapshot, including the explicitly pending PR159 Q-ball export; it does
not claim every referenced module is merged at this publication base.

Declared SI inputs in the material and LC models are useful accounting contracts,
not independently measured calibration. Canonical ticks, synthetic drive periods
and Q-ball model time are not interchangeable clocks. Ratios cannot recover
missing absolute initial energy, charge or length references.

Before any physical cross-system conversion, choose a justified subsystem pair
and provide each required base scale with its source, uncertainty and validity
range. Keep declared synthetic, independently measured and unavailable values
distinct. The dimensional formulas in the audit are consequences of supplied
scales; they neither select constants nor establish a physical correspondence.
