## What has been established

The engine now independently produces:

- a Hamiltonian gauge field;
- exact local U(1) symmetry;
- a Gauss constraint;
- an electric-like conjugate field;
- gauge-invariant curvature;
- propagating weak-field normal modes;
- an exact discrete dispersion law;
- an emergent dimensionless long-wave speed (sqrt{eta}).

The normal-mode relation follows from the implemented gauge Hamiltonian, not from insertion of Maxwell's wave equation.

## What remains

To turn this into a physical electromagnetic prediction the theory still needs an independent determination of:

1. the physical meaning of one routing interval (a);
2. the physical meaning of one evolution unit (	au);
3. the gauge stiffness (eta), unless fixed by a separate microscopic argument;
4. the mapping from routing/scale directions to actual physical geometry.

Only then can the predicted dimensional propagation speed be compared with (c).
