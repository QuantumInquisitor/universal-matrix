# Generated harmonics in a bounded wave model

This independent P05 experiment addresses a limitation explicitly recorded by
the recovered two-mode wave report: omitted third harmonics. The older sources
were located in draft PR120 at commit
`615cee59c51df77406555ac56d40e8a1f1e73ec8`; their recorded hashes reconcile after
explicit newline reconstruction. `recovery/wave-source-provenance.json` records
the evidence and limits. This is a separately declared extension, not an exact
continuation of that report's parameters or source implementation.

The recovered controller robustness artifact already includes force limits,
delays, measurement disturbance and stiffness mismatch. Its case settings match
the published reporter, but that artifact lacks source hashes. Do not classify
those controls as never run, or treat their exact generation provenance as proven.

## Model and energy normalization

Use `u(x,t)=sum q_n(t) cos(n*x)` on a periodic coordinate `x in [0,2*pi)`,
with odd cosine truncations `{1}`, `{1,3}` and `{1,3,5}`. These are standing-wave
coordinates in dimensionless model units. There is no calibrated physical
wavelength, material medium, quantum state or traveling-wave claim.

The cosine average squared norm is one half. Dividing the projected equation
by that norm gives unit modal mass, cubic force
`2*beta*mean(cos(n*x)*u^3)` and shared quartic potential `beta*mean(u^4)/2`.
Thus reported total energy is twice the spatially averaged field energy.
Sixty-four evenly spaced points resolve the quartic integrands here (maximum
spatial frequency 20); an independent 257-point check agrees.

```
k_n(t) = n^2 * (1+h*cos(Omega*t))
q_n'' = -k_n(t)*q_n - dUquartic/dq_n - 2*gamma*q_n'
E = sum_n [q_n'^2 + k_n(t)*q_n^2]/2 + Uquartic
Wdot = -h*Omega*sin(Omega*t)*sum_n(n^2*q_n^2)/2
Ddot = 2*gamma*sum_n(q_n'^2)
E(t)-E(0) = W(t)-D(t)
```

The pump changes stiffness uniformly; it does not add a third- or fifth-harmonic
forcing term. Parameters are beta=1, gamma=0.02, h=0.2 and Omega=2. Initially
q1=0.1, higher harmonics and all velocities/ledgers are zero. Twenty pump
periods are sampled at 201 common times. A finer three-mode run and a pump-off
three-mode control complete the five cases. The integration step/tolerance
refinement is separate from changing the number of retained modes.

## Results and interpretation

With modes 1, 3 and 5 retained, their largest sampled coordinate magnitudes are
0.3999113, 0.00926230 and 0.000186143. Higher harmonics begin at zero and are
generated by the cubic coupling. The independent identity
`cos(x)^3=(3*cos(x)+cos(3*x))/4` checks this mechanism directly. Even with the
pump off, harmonic 3 reaches a sampled magnitude 0.000103792 while total
energy decreases and pump work remains zero: harmonic generation itself does
not imply an energy source or a sustained pattern.

Adding harmonic 3 changes q1 by at most 9.2553e-4 at the shared samples. Adding
harmonic 5 changes q1 by 1.5914e-7 and q3 by 4.9619e-6. Finer integration of
the same three-mode model changes coordinates by at most 1.624e-12 and rates
by 8.043e-12. This separates a measured truncation effect from solver error
for these cases; it does not prove convergence to the full field equation.
Reported per-mode quadratic energies omit both pump stiffness and the shared
quartic energy and must not be summed as the complete energy budget.

Twenty-four tests passed. The largest energy-account residual across all cases
is 1.52e-13 model energy units; the finer case is below 7.15e-16. Controls include
an independent potential gradient, directional energy derivative, deliberately
omitted pump work, basis normalization, resolved quadrature and pump-off decay.

## Reproduction and remaining work

```sh
uv run --extra scientific python scripts/report_wave_harmonics.py --output artifacts/wave-harmonics/report.json
```

The report hashes its source and the tracked provenance manifest. Legacy
artifacts are not required to reproduce the new experiment; their hashes are
inherited from that manifest rather than reverified during a new run.

Multiple wavelengths, sine/phase degrees of freedom, different boundaries,
noise/seed ensembles, longer-time behavior and additional mode counts remain
open. This run does not establish unique pattern selection, a time-crystal
component benefit, or coupling to the material specimen.
